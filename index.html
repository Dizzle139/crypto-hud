Spot vs Perps ⓘ

Coinbase + OKX · taker flow · basis · funding
Spot is the asset itself changing hands — you buy a coin, you own it. Perpetual futures ("perps") are leveraged bets on the price that never expire: no coin moves, and a trader can hold far more exposure than the collateral they put up. Perps routinely trade several times the volume of spot, so most of what looks like price action is derivatives. This section exists to tell the two apart. A move paid for with spot buying has real money behind it and tends to hold; a move carried by leverage is borrowed, costs funding to keep on, and unwinds a great deal faster when it turns.
